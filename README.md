# Global Intelligence Agency GitHub Repositories

A verified list of GitHub organizations managed by national intelligence and cybersecurity agencies. These links are current as of May 2026.

## 🇺🇸 United States
* **National Security Agency (NSA)**
    * [NationalSecurityAgency](https://github.com/NationalSecurityAgency) — Home of Ghidra.
    * [nsacyber](https://github.com/nsacyber) — Cybersecurity defensive tools.
* **National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA)**
    * [ngageoint](https://github.com/ngageoint) — Over 100 geospatial and analysis tools.
* **Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA)**
    * [cisagov](https://github.com/cisagov) — Defensive tools like Malcolm and LME.
* **National Reconnaissance Office (NRO)**
    * [NRO-Open-Source](https://github.com/NRO-Open-Source) — Satellite and imaging utilities.
* **Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI)**
    * [IntelligenceCommunity](https://github.com/IntelligenceCommunity) — Community-wide standards and tools.
* **Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)**
    * [fbi](https://github.com/fbi) — Digital forensics and technical mirrors.

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
* **Government Communications Headquarters (GCHQ)**
    * [gchq](https://github.com/gchq) — Home of CyberChef and Gaffer.
* **Secret Intelligence Service (MI6)**
    * [mi6](https://github.com/mi6) — Intelligence Community UI kits and design systems.

## 🇨🇦 Canada
* **Canadian Centre for Cyber Security (CCCS / CSE)**
    * [Cyber-Centre](https://github.com/Cyber-Centre) — Home of Assemblyline.

## 🇦🇺 Australia
* **Australian Signals Directorate (ASD / ACSC)**
    * [asdcyber](https://github.com/asdcyber) — Australian Cyber Security Centre tools (e.g., Azul).

## 🇫🇷 France
* **National Cybersecurity Agency of France (ANSSI)**
    * [ANSSI-FR](https://github.com/ANSSI-FR) — Hardened OS (ClipOS) and security tools.

## 🇩🇪 Germany
* **Federal Office for Information Security (BSI)**
    * [BSI-Bund](https://github.com/BSI-Bund) — Standards and forensic tools (RdpCacheStitcher).
* **Federal Intelligence Service (BND)**
    * [BND-Bund](https://github.com/BND-Bund) — Declassified and open-source projects.

## 🇪🇺 European Union
* **European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA)**
    * [enisa-eu](https://github.com/enisa-eu) — Threat landscape and policy frameworks.
